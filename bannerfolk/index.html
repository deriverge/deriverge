Jak se hraje

1Sestav

Vyber 8 karet z 23 jednotek a 6 kouzel v 6 frakcích. Oddíly stejné frakce spolu odemykají bonusy.

2Rozestav

Každé kolo přinese víc zlata. Přidávej jednotky, přesouvej je, vylepšuj a miř kouzla.

3Sleduj

Bitva se odehraje sama. Přeživší poškodí hrad soupeře. Kdo ho první zboří, vyhrává, nejvýš po 10 kolech.

Asynchronous PvP strategy

Deploy your army, outsmart real players

Pick 8 cards, place your units on your half of the battlefield each round and watch the battle unfold on its own. Survivors strike the enemy castle. Bring it down first to win.

Coming soon to the App Store and Google Play

How to play

1Build

Choose 8 cards from 23 units and 6 spells in 6 factions. Units of the same faction unlock bonuses together.

2Deploy

Every round brings more gold. Place new units, move them around, level them up and aim your spells.

3Watch

The battle plays out by itself. Survivors damage the enemy castle. The first to destroy it wins, at most 10 rounds.

Ways to play

Ranked

Face the recorded armies of real players with similar trophies. Matches start instantly, no waiting.

Daily Defense

The same enemy and budget for everyone today. Win and save gold for a better score.

Weekly Siege

A strong commander of the week, the same for everyone, with its own leaderboard.

Friend Duel

Share a 6-character code and your friend fights your last recorded army. No trophies at stake.

Fair by design

  • No pay-to-win: cards have no levels outside a match
  • No loot boxes, chests, timers or energy
  • Purchases are cosmetic only, with one clear price
  • Ads only after a match, never during one, and no banners
  • Reward videos only when you ask for them
  • Robot opponents are always clearly marked

Seasons and rankings

Every 6 weeks a new season brings a free and a premium reward track of 20 tiers. Daily and weekly quests, and leaderboards for trophies, weekly wins, Daily Defense and Weekly Siege. In 35 languages.
