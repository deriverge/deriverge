What a UK invoice must include

GOV.UK sets out what an invoice has to show. For a tradesman that means:

  • a unique invoice number,
  • your business name and address (as a sole trader, your own name and any business name you use),
  • the customer's name or business name and address,
  • a clear description of the work and materials,
  • the date the work was done (date of supply) and the date of the invoice,
  • the amount charged for each item and the total amount due.

A limited company must use its full name as it appears on the certificate of incorporation. If you show the names of your directors, you have to show all of them.

When you are VAT registered

A VAT invoice also needs your VAT registration number, the time of supply (tax point), the VAT rate for each item, the amount of VAT, and the totals with and without VAT. The standard rate is 20%. Some building work, such as installing certain energy saving materials or converting and renovating empty homes, can have a reduced or zero rate. Check HMRC's guidance for the job before you use it.

You must register for VAT when your taxable turnover goes over £90,000 in a rolling 12 month period (threshold from April 2024).

If you are a VAT registered subcontractor working for a VAT registered contractor under CIS, the construction domestic reverse charge may apply. The invoice then says that the customer accounts for the VAT, instead of you charging it.

Getting paid on time

  • Payment terms: put them on every invoice, for example 14 or 30 days.
  • Bank details: account name, sort code and account number, and ask customers to use the invoice number as the payment reference.
  • Late payment: with business customers you can usually claim statutory interest of 8% above the Bank of England base rate plus a fixed sum for each late invoice.
  • Card payments: in the Fixbill app you can add your own PayPal or Stripe payment link, and the invoice gets a QR code to pay it.

Questions people ask

Do I need to be VAT registered to send an invoice?

No. If you are not VAT registered you invoice without VAT and must not show any. The template has a “Not VAT registered” option.

What payment terms can I set?

Whatever you agree with your customer; 14 or 30 days is common. Put the terms and a due date on every invoice.
