Privacy Policy

Fixbill · effective 6 October 2026 · operator: deriverge s.r.o. · contact: info@deriverge.com

Fixbill helps you create quotes and invoices on your phone. Short version: your business data stays on your device. No account, no analytics, no ads, no tracking.

1. Your data stays on your device

  • Everything you enter in Fixbill is stored only on your device: your business profile, clients, items and price list, quotes and invoices, photos and signatures. None of it is sent to us, and we have no access to it.
  • Fixbill has no user accounts. You do not sign up or log in anywhere.
  • A document leaves your device only when you share it yourself, for example by e-mail or in a messenger. It then goes to the app or service you pick, under that service's own terms.
  • Backups are files that you create and save where you choose, for example in Files, on a cloud drive or in an e-mail to yourself. They contain your business data, so keep them safe.
  • Your clients' details (such as names, addresses and e-mail addresses) also stay only on your device. We have no access to them.

2. Company lookup

Next to the company ID and VAT number fields there is a lookup button. Only when you tap it does the app send that one number to a public registry and fill in the company details the registry returns:

  • ARES, the register of economic subjects run by the Czech Ministry of Finance, for Czech company IDs (IČO);
  • the French public company search API (recherche-entreprises.api.gouv.fr) for SIREN and SIRET numbers;
  • VIES, the VAT number validation service of the European Commission, for EU VAT numbers.

Nothing else is sent. As with any connection over the internet, the registry can see your device's IP address.

3. Camera and photos

Fixbill asks for access only when you use a feature that needs it: the camera to take photos of the job, and your photos to add a picture or your logo to a document. You can change this access any time in your device settings.

4. What we do not collect

  • No account: we do not know your name, e-mail address or phone number.
  • No analytics, no ads, no tracking, and no third-party tools that collect data, apart from the subscription check described below.
  • We do not sell any data and do not share data for advertising.
  • Apple and Google may give us anonymous, aggregated statistics and crash reports from people who allowed sharing with developers in their device settings. The app itself does not collect them.

5. Subscriptions (Fixbill Pro)

Fixbill Pro is an auto-renewable subscription. Payments are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) under their own terms and privacy policies. We never see your payment details.

To verify your subscription and restore it (for example after reinstalling the app or on a new phone), the app uses RevenueCat, a service of RevenueCat, Inc. RevenueCat receives only the store's purchase receipt or token and a random, anonymous app user ID created by the app: no name, no e-mail address, no business data. RevenueCat, Inc. is based in the USA, so this data may be processed there. The legal basis is performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). More at revenuecat.com/privacy.

6. How long data is kept

  • Data in the app stays on your device until you delete it. Uninstalling the app deletes all of it. We cannot restore it, because we never have a copy, so keep a backup.
  • Backup files stay wherever you saved them until you delete them.
  • RevenueCat keeps the purchase record as long as it is needed to verify and restore your subscription. You can ask us to delete it.
  • If you e-mail us, we keep your message only as long as needed to handle your request.

7. Children

Fixbill is a tool for businesses and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

8. Your rights

The controller of your personal data is deriverge s.r.o. (info@deriverge.com). Under the GDPR you have the right to access your personal data, to have it corrected or erased, to restrict or object to its processing, and to data portability. You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection authority, for example in your country or, in the Czech Republic, with the Office for Personal Data Protection (ÚOOÚ).

Your business data is on your device, so you control it directly. For anything else, including the purchase record at RevenueCat, write to info@deriverge.com. Because we do not know who you are, we may ask for details from your store receipt to find the right record.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we will publish the new version on this page with a new effective date. See also the terms of use.
