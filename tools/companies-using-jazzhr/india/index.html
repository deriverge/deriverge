about the data

About JazzHR and this list

JazzHR is applicant tracking software for small and mid-sized businesses, most of them in the United States. Each company's career site lives at <company>.applytojob.com.

How this list is made. We find JazzHR career sites in the public Common Crawl web archive and read each board's public job list every day for our job index. A company's country is the one where most of its open jobs are located, so a company that hires in several countries appears once, under its main one. Companies with no openings today have no known country and are left out here; they are in the full JazzHR list. This is a sample of employers we could find, not JazzHR's full customer list in India. For the jobs themselves, across all 21 systems, see job postings data for India.

The list holds company names and public career pages only, no personal data. If your company should not be here, write to info@deriverge.com.

Frequently asked questions

How many companies in India use JazzHR?

We know of 59 companies on JazzHR that hire mainly in India and had open jobs on 20 November 2026. The real number is higher: companies with no openings today, an unlisted board or most of their jobs in another country are not counted here.

Which companies in India use JazzHR?

By open jobs on 20 November 2026, the largest are Ebizon, Pipecare Group, Neolytix, ProcDNA, Asian Heart Institute. The table above lists all 59 with a link to each careers page.

How is the country of a company decided?

We read the locations of all open jobs on the company's board and take the country that appears most often. A company that hires in several countries is listed once, under its main one.

Can I download this list?

Yes. Open the full JazzHR list, where India is already selected, and press Download CSV. You get company, careers page, website where known, main country and open jobs. You may reuse the list under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 licence with a link to this page.

How do I get the open jobs of these companies?

Run a job scraper on Apify with the country filter set to India: it searches all JazzHR jobs in our daily index and returns titles, locations, descriptions and apply links.
