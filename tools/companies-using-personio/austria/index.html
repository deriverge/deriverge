about the data

About Personio and this list

Personio is HR software from Munich for small and mid-sized companies in Europe, with recruiting built in. Job pages live at <company>.jobs.personio.de or .com.

How this list is made. We find Personio career sites in the public Common Crawl web archive and read each board's public job list every day for our job index. A company's country is the one where most of its open jobs are located, so a company that hires in several countries appears once, under its main one. Companies with no openings today have no known country and are left out here; they are in the full Personio list. This is a sample of employers we could find, not Personio's full customer list in Austria. For the jobs themselves, across all 21 systems, see job postings data for Austria.

The list holds company names and public career pages only, no personal data. If your company should not be here, write to info@deriverge.com.

Frequently asked questions

How many companies in Austria use Personio?

We know of 38 companies on Personio that hire mainly in Austria and had open jobs on 20 November 2026. The real number is higher: companies with no openings today, an unlisted board or most of their jobs in another country are not counted here.

Which companies in Austria use Personio?

By open jobs on 20 November 2026, the largest are DENZEL Gruppe, EurimPharm Produktions GmbH, Chaka2 GmbH, Voltida Energieprojekte GmbH, Transped Europe GmbH. The table above lists all 38 with a link to each careers page.

How is the country of a company decided?

We read the locations of all open jobs on the company's board and take the country that appears most often. A company that hires in several countries is listed once, under its main one.

Can I download this list?

Yes. Open the full Personio list, where Austria is already selected, and press Download CSV. You get company, careers page, website where known, main country and open jobs. You may reuse the list under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 licence with a link to this page.

How do I get the open jobs of these companies?

Run a job scraper on Apify with the country filter set to Austria: it searches all Personio jobs in our daily index and returns titles, locations, descriptions and apply links.
