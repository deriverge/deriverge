about the data

About Rippling and this list

Rippling's recruiting module is part of its HR, IT and payroll platform. Job boards live at ats.rippling.com/<company>/jobs.

How this list is made. We find Rippling career sites in the public Common Crawl web archive and read each board's public job list every day for our job index. A company's country is the one where most of its open jobs are located, so a company that hires in several countries appears once, under its main one. Companies with no openings today have no known country and are left out here; they are in the full Rippling list. This is a sample of employers we could find, not Rippling's full customer list in Canada. For the jobs themselves, across all 21 systems, see job postings data for Canada.

The list holds company names and public career pages only, no personal data. If your company should not be here, write to info@deriverge.com.

Frequently asked questions

How many companies in Canada use Rippling?

We know of 75 companies on Rippling that hire mainly in Canada and had open jobs on 20 November 2026. The real number is higher: companies with no openings today, an unlisted board or most of their jobs in another country are not counted here.

Which companies in Canada use Rippling?

By open jobs on 20 November 2026, the largest are nesto, Nesto Group(e), Dialogue, exacare ai, Dialogue. The table above lists all 75 with a link to each careers page.

How is the country of a company decided?

We read the locations of all open jobs on the company's board and take the country that appears most often. A company that hires in several countries is listed once, under its main one.

Can I download this list?

Yes. Open the full Rippling list, where Canada is already selected, and press Download CSV. You get company, careers page, website where known, main country and open jobs. You may reuse the list under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 licence with a link to this page.

How do I get the open jobs of these companies?

Run a job scraper on Apify with the country filter set to Canada: it searches all Rippling jobs in our daily index and returns titles, locations, descriptions and apply links.
