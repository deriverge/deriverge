Frequently asked questions

How many job postings are there in Canada?

Our index of 11 November 2026 held 64,223 open jobs in Canada from 4,656 employer career sites, with 14,656 first seen in the last 7 days. It covers employers that publish jobs on 20 applicant tracking systems, so it is a large sample of the market, not all of it: jobs posted only on job boards or social networks are not included.

Which employers have the most open jobs in Canada?

By open jobs on 11 November 2026: HealthCareersInSask.ca, Cornerstone Protection Group, ACML AgeCare Management Ltd.. The table above lists the top 12.

How can I get all job postings in Canada as a dataset?

All 64,223 open jobs in Canada fit the Scale plan at $99 a month: the 20,000 newest open jobs on the first day, then about 2,094 new jobs a day, with descriptions, added and closed files, XML for job board software and your own feed page. A narrower selection of up to 100 new jobs a day, for one city, job category or level, is the Starter plan at $29 a month. The data comes as JSON Lines and CSV files at a private link that updates every morning, or through an API. Write to info@deriverge.com for a sample of your exact feed.

Is there a free sample?

Yes. This CSV holds 100 of the newest jobs in Canada with title, employer, location, work arrangement, category, level, dates, system and job link. The job data feed page has a larger sample across all systems.

How fresh is the data?

The index is rebuilt every night. Boards on ten systems are read daily and the other four within one to three days, so a new job reaches the data within three days. This page shows the index of 11 November 2026.

Does the data contain personal data?

No. The records describe jobs and employers: there is no candidate data and no list of recruiters. Descriptions, delivered only on request, are the employer's own text; in feeds we remove email addresses and phone numbers from them.
