Frequently asked questions

How many job postings are there in the United Arab Emirates?

Our index of 20 November 2026 held 9,925 open jobs in the United Arab Emirates from 877 employer career sites, with 1,074 first seen in the last 7 days. It covers employers that publish jobs on 21 applicant tracking systems, so it is a large sample of the market, not all of it: jobs posted only on job boards or social networks are not included.

Which employers have the most open jobs in the United Arab Emirates?

By open jobs on 20 November 2026: Ali & Sons Holding LLC, LANDMARK GROUP, AccorHotel. The table above lists the top 12.

Is there a free sample?

Yes. This CSV holds 100 of the newest jobs in the United Arab Emirates with title, employer, location, work arrangement, category, level, dates, system and job link. The job data feed page has a larger sample across all systems.

How fresh is the data?

The index is rebuilt every night. Boards on ten systems are read daily and the other four within one to three days, so a new job reaches the data within three days. This page shows the index of 20 November 2026.

Does the data contain personal data?

No. The records describe jobs and employers: there is no candidate data and no list of recruiters. Descriptions, delivered only on request, are the employer's own text; in feeds we remove email addresses and phone numbers from them.
