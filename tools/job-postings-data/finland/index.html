questions

Frequently asked questions

How many job postings are there in Finland?

Our index of 25 November 2026 held 1,643 open jobs in Finland from 325 employer career sites, with 223 first seen in the last 7 days. It covers employers that publish jobs on 21 applicant tracking systems, so it is a large sample of the market, not all of it: jobs posted only on job boards or social networks are not included.

Which employers have the most open jobs in Finland?

By open jobs on 25 November 2026: StaffMill, ICEYE, HUS Urasivusto. The table above lists the top 12.

How can I get all job postings in Finland as a dataset?

All 1,643 open jobs in Finland fit the Starter plan at $29 a month: every open job on the first day, then about 32 new jobs a day, with descriptions, added and closed files, XML for job board software and your own feed page. The data comes as JSON Lines and CSV files at a private link that updates every morning, or through an API. Write to info@deriverge.com for a sample of your exact feed.

Is there a free sample?

Yes. This CSV holds 100 of the newest jobs in Finland with title, employer, location, work arrangement, category, level, dates, system and job link. The job data feed page has a larger sample across all systems.

How fresh is the data?

The index is rebuilt every night. Boards on ten systems are read daily and the other four within one to three days, so a new job reaches the data within three days. This page shows the index of 25 November 2026.

Does the data contain personal data?

No. The records describe jobs and employers: there is no candidate data and no list of recruiters. Descriptions, delivered only on request, are the employer's own text; in feeds we remove email addresses and phone numbers from them.
