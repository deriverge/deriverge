questions

Frequently asked questions

How many job postings are there in the Netherlands?

Our index of 9 November 2026 held 16,247 open jobs in the Netherlands from 1,653 employer career sites, with 1,819 first seen in the last 7 days. It covers employers that publish jobs on 14 applicant tracking systems, so it is a large sample of the market, not all of it: jobs posted only on job boards or social networks are not included.

Which employers have the most open jobs in the Netherlands?

By open jobs on 9 November 2026: Deloitte Netherlands, aaff B.V., JYSK. The table above lists the top 12.

How can I get all job postings in the Netherlands as a dataset?

All 16,247 open jobs in the Netherlands come in the Filtered feed from $299 a month, a daily file with the jobs added and closed that day (about 260 new a day). A narrower selection of up to 100 new jobs a day, for one city, job category or level, is the Niche feed at $99 a month. The data comes as JSON Lines and CSV files at a private link that updates every morning, or through an API. Write to info@deriverge.com for a sample of your exact feed.

Is there a free sample?

Yes. This CSV holds 100 of the newest jobs in the Netherlands with title, employer, location, work arrangement, category, level, dates, system and job link. The job data feed page has a larger sample across all systems.

How fresh is the data?

The index is rebuilt every night. Boards on ten systems are read daily and the other four within one to three days, so a new job reaches the data within three days. This page shows the index of 9 November 2026.

Does the data contain personal data?

No. The records describe jobs and employers: there is no candidate data and no list of recruiters. Descriptions, delivered only on request, are the employer's own text; in feeds we remove email addresses and phone numbers from them.
