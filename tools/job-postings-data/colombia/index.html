Frequently asked questions

How many job postings are there in Colombia?

Our index of 20 November 2026 held 5,138 open jobs in Colombia from 752 employer career sites, with 407 first seen in the last 7 days. It covers employers that publish jobs on 21 applicant tracking systems, so it is a large sample of the market, not all of it: jobs posted only on job boards or social networks are not included.

Which employers have the most open jobs in Colombia?

By open jobs on 20 November 2026: Wood, Inetum, Pavago. The table above lists the top 12.

How can I get all job postings in Colombia as a dataset?

All 5,138 open jobs in Colombia fit the Starter plan at $29 a month: the 5,000 newest open jobs on the first day, then about 58 new jobs a day, with descriptions, added and closed files, XML for job board software and your own feed page. The data comes as JSON Lines and CSV files at a private link that updates every morning, or through an API. Write to info@deriverge.com for a sample of your exact feed.

Is there a free sample?

Yes. This CSV holds 100 of the newest jobs in Colombia with title, employer, location, work arrangement, category, level, dates, system and job link. The job data feed page has a larger sample across all systems.

How fresh is the data?

The index is rebuilt every night. Boards on ten systems are read daily and the other four within one to three days, so a new job reaches the data within three days. This page shows the index of 20 November 2026.

Does the data contain personal data?

No. The records describe jobs and employers: there is no candidate data and no list of recruiters. Descriptions, delivered only on request, are the employer's own text; in feeds we remove email addresses and phone numbers from them.
