Frequently asked questions

How many job postings are there in Bulgaria?

Our index of 10 November 2026 held 2,143 open jobs in Bulgaria from 351 employer career sites, with 277 first seen in the last 7 days. It covers employers that publish jobs on 18 applicant tracking systems, so it is a large sample of the market, not all of it: jobs posted only on job boards or social networks are not included.

Which employers have the most open jobs in Bulgaria?

By open jobs on 10 November 2026: Mercier Consultancy Group, Next Job Abroad, Tieto. The table above lists the top 12.

How can I get all job postings in Bulgaria as a dataset?

All 2,143 open jobs in Bulgaria fit the Starter plan at $29 a month: every open job on the first day, then about 40 new jobs a day, with descriptions, added and closed files, XML for job board software and your own feed page. The data comes as JSON Lines and CSV files at a private link that updates every morning, or through an API. Write to info@deriverge.com for a sample of your exact feed.

Is there a free sample?

Yes. This CSV holds 100 of the newest jobs in Bulgaria with title, employer, location, work arrangement, category, level, dates, system and job link. The job data feed page has a larger sample across all systems.

How fresh is the data?

The index is rebuilt every night. Boards on ten systems are read daily and the other four within one to three days, so a new job reaches the data within three days. This page shows the index of 10 November 2026.

Does the data contain personal data?

No. The records describe jobs and employers: there is no candidate data and no list of recruiters. Descriptions, delivered only on request, are the employer's own text; in feeds we remove email addresses and phone numbers from them.
