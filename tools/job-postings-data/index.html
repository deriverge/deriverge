fields

What each job record holds

Each job is one record: title, employer and its website, career site and apply link, locations with ISO country codes, remote, hybrid or onsite, employment type, level, one of 21 job categories, department, salary as numbers when the ad states it, posting date and the day our index first saw the job. The full description comes on request, as plain text and clean HTML. Daily files list the jobs added and the jobs closed, so a job board can take closed jobs down the same day.

The jobs come from the public job boards that employers run on Workday, Greenhouse, Lever, SmartRecruiters, Ashby, iCIMS, Oracle Recruiting Cloud and seven more systems. See the systems and the employers on each. There is no candidate data and no list of recruiters.

Frequently asked questions

What is in this job postings dataset?

Every open job our index finds on the public career sites of 53,852 employers on 20 applicant tracking systems: 1,964,604 jobs on 11 November 2026, in 167 countries. Each job has its title, employer, location and country, work arrangement, category, level, salary when stated, dates and the apply link on the employer's site.

How often is the data updated?

Every night. Boards on ten systems are read daily and the other four within one to three days. Each daily file lists the jobs added and closed since the day before.

Can I download job postings data for free?

Yes, a sample: every country page has a CSV with 100 of its newest jobs, and the job data feed page has a larger sample across all systems. The complete data for a country, a category or the world comes as a daily feed.

How much does the data cost?

Starter, up to 100 new jobs a day picked for your audience, is $29 a month; Growth, up to 2,000 new jobs a day, is $59; Scale, up to 20,000, is $99; and the full daily file of every open job is $299 a month. Every feed starts with a free two-week trial. Pay-per-request access runs through our API on Apify and RapidAPI.

Who uses job postings data?

Job boards and aggregators that need fresh jobs for their audience, HR tech products, recruiters looking for companies that hire, and labour market research on demand by country, skill or level.

Does the data contain personal data?

No. The records describe jobs and employers only. Descriptions are the employer's own text; in feeds we remove email addresses and phone numbers from them.
