remote by country

Remote jobs open to one country

A remote job board for people in one country needs jobs they can actually take. These feeds keep a remote job only when its location names the country, its region or says worldwide, and its description does not limit it to other countries (“must be based in the US”, “authorized to work in the UK”). At most three new jobs a day come from one employer.

questions

Frequently asked questions

How many remote jobs are in our data?

Our index of 11 November 2026 held 135,184 open remote jobs from 14,363 employer career sites, with 23,630 first seen in the last 7 days. It covers employers that publish jobs on 20 applicant tracking systems, so it is a large sample of the market, not all of it: jobs posted only on job boards or social networks are not included.

Which employers have the most open remote jobs?

By open jobs on 11 November 2026: Prolific Academic Ltd, Bjak, AO Globe Life. The table above lists the top 12.

How can I get all remote jobs as a dataset?

All 135,184 open remote jobs fit the Scale plan at $99 a month: the 20,000 newest open jobs on the first day, then about 3,376 new jobs a day, with descriptions, added and closed files, XML for job board software and your own feed page. A narrower selection of up to 100 new jobs a day, for one city, job category or level, is the Starter plan at $29 a month. The data comes as JSON Lines and CSV files at a private link that updates every morning, or through an API. Write to info@deriverge.com for a sample of your exact feed.

Is there a free sample?

Yes. This CSV holds 100 of the newest remote jobs with title, employer, location, work arrangement, category, level, dates, system and job link. The job data feed page has a larger sample across all systems.

How fresh is the data?

The index is rebuilt every night. Boards on ten systems are read daily and the other four within one to three days, so a new job reaches the data within three days. This page shows the index of 11 November 2026.

Does the data contain personal data?

No. The records describe jobs and employers: there is no candidate data and no list of recruiters. Descriptions, delivered only on request, are the employer's own text; in feeds we remove email addresses and phone numbers from them.
