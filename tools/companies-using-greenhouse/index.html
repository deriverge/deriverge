// check it yourself

How to tell if a company uses Greenhouse

Open any job on the company's careers page and look where the Apply button leads. A company uses Greenhouse when:

  • Apply links go to boards.greenhouse.io/<token> or job-boards.greenhouse.io/<token>.
  • Job links on the company's own site end with ?gh_jid=<number>.
  • The page source loads boards.greenhouse.io/embed/job_board/js?for=<token>.

The public job feed of a Greenhouse board is at https://boards-api.greenhouse.io/v1/boards/<token>/jobs. It needs no key and is what our index reads every day.

Not sure which system a link belongs to? Paste it into the ATS detector, or search the company by name on the ATS overview.

// about the data

About Greenhouse and this list

Greenhouse is a recruiting platform from New York, common among venture-backed technology companies and scale-ups. Its job boards live at job-boards.greenhouse.io and publish a public JSON API, which is how every company on this list is read.

How this list is made. We find Greenhouse career sites in the public Common Crawl web archive and read each board's public job list every day for our job index. A company is listed when its board answered with a company name; companies with a live board but no openings today are included with 0 jobs. The main country is the country with most of the company's job locations. This is a sample of employers we could find, not Greenhouse's full customer list. The list holds company names and public career pages only, no personal data. If your company should not be here, write to info@deriverge.com.
