about the data

About Ashby and this list

Ashby is an all-in-one recruiting platform popular with fast-growing startups. Job boards live at jobs.ashbyhq.com and most of them link to the company website, which is where the website column comes from.

How this list is made. We find Ashby career sites in the public Common Crawl web archive and read each board's public job list every day for our job index. A company's country is the one where most of its open jobs are located, so a company that hires in several countries appears once, under its main one. Companies with no openings today have no known country and are left out here; they are in the full Ashby list. This is a sample of employers we could find, not Ashby's full customer list in India. For the jobs themselves, across all 21 systems, see job postings data for India.

The list holds company names and public career pages only, no personal data. If your company should not be here, write to info@deriverge.com.

Frequently asked questions

How many companies in India use Ashby?

We know of 55 companies on Ashby that hire mainly in India and had open jobs on 25 November 2026. The real number is higher: companies with no openings today, an unlisted board or most of their jobs in another country are not counted here.

Which companies in India use Ashby?

By open jobs on 25 November 2026, the largest are LILT (Production), Tekion, Riveron, NETGEAR, SurveyMonkey. The table above lists all 55 with a link to each careers page.

How is the country of a company decided?

We read the locations of all open jobs on the company's board and take the country that appears most often. A company that hires in several countries is listed once, under its main one.

Can I download this list?

Yes. Open the full Ashby list, where India is already selected, and press Download CSV. You get company, careers page, website where known, main country and open jobs. You may reuse the list under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 licence with a link to this page.

How do I get the open jobs of these companies?

Run a job scraper on Apify with the country filter set to India: it searches all Ashby jobs in our daily index and returns titles, locations, descriptions and apply links.
