about the data

About SmartRecruiters and this list

SmartRecruiters is a talent acquisition platform used by large and mid-sized employers. Company career pages live at careers.smartrecruiters.com/<Company> and individual jobs at jobs.smartrecruiters.com.

How this list is made. We find SmartRecruiters career sites in the public Common Crawl web archive and read each board's public job list every day for our job index. A company's country is the one where most of its open jobs are located, so a company that hires in several countries appears once, under its main one. Companies with no openings today have no known country and are left out here; they are in the full SmartRecruiters list. This is a sample of employers we could find, not SmartRecruiters's full customer list in the United States. For the jobs themselves, across all 21 systems, see job postings data for the United States.

The list holds company names and public career pages only, no personal data. If your company should not be here, write to info@deriverge.com.

Frequently asked questions

How many companies in the United States use SmartRecruiters?

We know of 1,559 companies on SmartRecruiters that hire mainly in the United States and had open jobs on 20 November 2026. The real number is higher: companies with no openings today, an unlisted board or most of their jobs in another country are not counted here.

Which companies in the United States use SmartRecruiters?

By open jobs on 20 November 2026, the largest are AECOM, CROSSMARK, Dev, Domino's, ProSidian Consulting, LLC. The table above lists the top 300 with a link to each careers page.

How is the country of a company decided?

We read the locations of all open jobs on the company's board and take the country that appears most often. A company that hires in several countries is listed once, under its main one.

Can I download this list?
