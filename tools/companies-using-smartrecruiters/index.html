// check it yourself

How to tell if a company uses SmartRecruiters

Open any job on the company's careers page and look where the Apply button leads. A company uses SmartRecruiters when:

  • The career page is careers.smartrecruiters.com/<Company>.
  • Job links look like jobs.smartrecruiters.com/<Company>/<id>.

The public job feed of a SmartRecruiters board is at https://api.smartrecruiters.com/v1/companies/<Company>/postings. It needs no key and is what our index reads every day.

Not sure which system a link belongs to? Paste it into the ATS detector, or search the company by name on the ATS overview.

// about the data

About SmartRecruiters and this list

SmartRecruiters is a talent acquisition platform used by large and mid-sized employers. Company career pages live at careers.smartrecruiters.com/<Company> and individual jobs at jobs.smartrecruiters.com.

How this list is made. We find SmartRecruiters career sites in the public Common Crawl web archive and read each board's public job list every day for our job index. A company is listed when its board answered with a company name; companies with a live board but no openings today are included with 0 jobs. The main country is the country with most of the company's job locations. This is a sample of employers we could find, not SmartRecruiters's full customer list. The list holds company names and public career pages only, no personal data. If your company should not be here, write to info@deriverge.com.
