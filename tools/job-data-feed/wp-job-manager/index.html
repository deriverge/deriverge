connect

Connect the feed to WP Job Manager

  1. Install WP All Import Pro and WP Job Manager's free WP All Import Integration add-on. The free WP All Import cannot import from an address on a schedule.
  2. Start a new import, choose to download the file from a URL and paste the address of your open.xml file.
  3. Import into Job Listings and drag our elements onto the WP Job Manager fields, using the mapping below.
  4. Use referencenumber as the unique identifier, so each daily run updates jobs instead of duplicating them.
  5. Turn on removing posts that are no longer in the import file, and schedule the import once a day.

questions

Frequently asked questions

Can WP Job Manager import jobs from an XML feed?

Yes. WP Job Manager's WP All Import reads a feed from its address (XML or CSV), on a schedule (cron or WP All Import's scheduling). It needs the WP All Import Pro and the free WP Job Manager add-on.

Where do the jobs in the feed come from?

From the public career sites of more than 47,000 employers on Workday, Greenhouse, Lever, SmartRecruiters, Ashby and thirteen more applicant tracking systems. Every job links to the apply page on the employer's own site. More on data sources and privacy.

Can I choose which jobs I get?

Yes. A feed can hold remote jobs open to one country, the jobs in a country or city, chosen job categories or levels, jobs at the companies you name, or every job in the index. We cap the new jobs per employer per day, so one large company does not fill your board.

Do closed jobs leave my WP Job Manager board?

A job that closes at the employer drops out of open.xml the next morning. WP Job Manager: Yes: "remove posts not present in this import file".

Can I send these jobs on to Indeed or other aggregators?

Keep them on your own board. Indeed and some other aggregators do not accept jobs that were collected from other sites.
