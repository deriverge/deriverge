Data sources and privacy

How the jobs in our data feed are collected, what the records hold and how we handle personal data and removal requests.

sources

Where the data comes from

Every job comes from a public job board that an employer publishes on its applicant tracking system, such as Workday, Greenhouse, Lever, SmartRecruiters, Ashby or iCIMS. We find these career sites through their addresses in the public Common Crawl web archive and read each board's public job list, most of them every day and the rest within three days.

  • No logins, no accounts and no paywalled content.
  • We respect robots.txt and leave out career sites that disallow their job feed.
  • We never bypass access controls, captchas or rate limits, and we stop reading a site that challenges our requests.
  • Requests are spread out so that no employer's site carries a noticeable load.

records

What the records hold

Each record describes one job and its employer: title, employer and website, locations with country codes, work arrangement, employment type, level, job category, salary when the ad states it, posting date, the day we first saw the job and links to the job and apply pages on the employer's own site. The full description, written by the employer, is delivered only on request.

There is no candidate data, no list of recruiters or employees and no contact enrichment.

privacy

Personal data and GDPR

deriverge s.r.o. is a company in the Czech Republic and follows the EU General Data Protection Regulation. The dataset is built from facts about jobs and employers, not about people.

An employer's own description can mention a hiring manager or a contact. For that reason descriptions are optional, and in every delivered feed we remove email addresses and phone numbers from them. We do not search, collect or sell information about the people named in job ads, and we do not combine the data with other sources about persons.

removal

Removal and corrections

An employer can ask us to remove its jobs or its company from our data at info@deriverge.com. We remove it from the index and from every customer feed, usually within two working days, and keep it out of future builds. The same address takes corrections and any question about a record.

customers

What we ask of customers

Customers decide how they use the data and are responsible for using it lawfully, in line with the employers' terms and any rules that apply to job advertising in their market. Every record links back to the job on the employer's own site, and we ask customers who publish jobs to keep that link, so candidates apply with the employer directly.
