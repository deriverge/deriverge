connect

Connect the feed to Jobiqo

  1. Open Jobs, then Job Importers, and click Add Feed.
  2. Paste the address of your open.xml file. Our link needs no authorization header.
  3. Map the fields and set the unpublish threshold, then save.

questions

Frequently asked questions

Can Jobiqo import jobs from an XML feed?

Yes. Jobiqo's Job Importers reads a feed from its address (XML), every few hours. It needs the Enterprise.

Where do the jobs in the feed come from?

From the public career sites of more than 47,000 employers on Workday, Greenhouse, Lever, SmartRecruiters, Ashby and nine more applicant tracking systems. Every job links to the apply page on the employer's own site. More on data sources and privacy.

Can I choose which jobs I get?

Yes. A feed can hold remote jobs open to one country, the jobs in a country or city, chosen job categories or levels, jobs at the companies you name, or every job in the index. We cap the new jobs per employer per day, so one large company does not fill your board.

Do closed jobs leave my Jobiqo board?

A job that closes at the employer drops out of open.xml the next morning. Jobiqo: Yes, with an unpublish threshold as a safety stop.

Can I send these jobs on to Indeed or other aggregators?

Keep them on your own board. Indeed and some other aggregators do not accept jobs that were collected from other sites.
