connect

Connect the feed to Cavuno

  1. Open Backfill, then Feeds, and click Add feed.
  2. Paste the address of your open.xml file.
  3. Set Check every (6 hours is the default; our file changes once a day, so 12 or 24 hours is enough).
  4. Turn on Expire missing jobs, so jobs that close at the employer leave your board.
  5. Review the field mapping Cavuno proposes on the first run and save.

tips

Limits and tips

  • Cavuno requires a title, a company and an apply URL or email; every job in our feed has all three.
  • A board can hold up to 10 feeds, and the plan's active-job limit applies (500 jobs on the smallest plan). Ask us to size your feed to fit.
  • If more than about 40% of the jobs vanish in one run, Cavuno pauses the feed for review instead of expiring them. A feed that changes once a day by a few percent never triggers it.

questions

Frequently asked questions

Can Cavuno import jobs from an XML feed?

Yes. Cavuno's recurring XML feeds reads a feed from its address (XML, also gzipped), every 1, 3, 6, 12 or 24 hours. It needs the backfill is part of the paid plans.

Where do the jobs in the feed come from?

From the public career sites of more than 69,000 employers on Workday, Greenhouse, Lever, SmartRecruiters, Ashby and fifteen more applicant tracking systems. Every job links to the apply page on the employer's own site. More on data sources and privacy.

Can I choose which jobs I get?

Yes. A feed can hold remote jobs open to one country, the jobs in a country or city, chosen job categories or levels, jobs at the companies you name, or every job in the index. We cap the new jobs per employer per day, so one large company does not fill your board.

Do closed jobs leave my Cavuno board?

A job that closes at the employer drops out of open.xml the next morning. Cavuno: Yes, optional: "Expire missing jobs" after two missed runs.

Can I send these jobs on to Indeed or other aggregators?

Keep them on your own board. Indeed and some other aggregators do not accept jobs that were collected from other sites.
