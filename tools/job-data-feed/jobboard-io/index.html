connect

Connect the feed to JobBoard.io

  1. Open Job Settings, then Auto Import, and click Create New Feed.
  2. Paste the address of your open.xml file and set the schedule to daily.
  3. JobBoard.io's default tags follow the same Indeed-style format as our file, so most fields match as they are. Leave the posting date unmapped (see the tip below).
  4. Turn on Auto Expire Jobs so closed jobs leave your board.

tips

Limits and tips

  • JobBoard.io expects a mapped posting date as MM/DD/YYYY, while our date uses the standard feed format. Leave the date unmapped and JobBoard.io uses the import date.

questions

Frequently asked questions

Can JobBoard.io import jobs from an XML feed?

Yes. JobBoard.io's Auto Import reads a feed from its address (XML), daily, weekly or monthly. It needs the Professional plan (five feeds).

Where do the jobs in the feed come from?

From the public career sites of more than 47,000 employers on Workday, Greenhouse, Lever, SmartRecruiters, Ashby and nine more applicant tracking systems. Every job links to the apply page on the employer's own site. More on data sources and privacy.

Can I choose which jobs I get?

Yes. A feed can hold remote jobs open to one country, the jobs in a country or city, chosen job categories or levels, jobs at the companies you name, or every job in the index. We cap the new jobs per employer per day, so one large company does not fill your board.

Do closed jobs leave my JobBoard.io board?

A job that closes at the employer drops out of open.xml the next morning. JobBoard.io: Yes, with "Auto Expire Jobs".

Can I send these jobs on to Indeed or other aggregators?

Keep them on your own board. Indeed and some other aggregators do not accept jobs that were collected from other sites.
