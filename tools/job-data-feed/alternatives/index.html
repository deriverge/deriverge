when us

When deriverge is the better choice

  • You run a job board and want jobs straight from employers. Every job links to the apply page on the employer's own site, with no aggregator in between, and the feed comes as XML that job board software imports as it is.
  • You need to take closed jobs down. The daily closed file is part of every plan, not a separate product.
  • You want a niche. Remote jobs open to one country, one country or city, one category: we build and check the selection, and cap the jobs per employer so one company does not fill your board.
  • You care about privacy. No recruiter or hiring manager contacts, and email addresses and phone numbers are removed from descriptions. deriverge is an EU company.
  • Price per job matters. $59 a month covers up to 2,000 new jobs a day.

when them

When another provider fits better

  • You need several years of history for analytics: TheirStack, Techmap, Coresignal and JobsPikr keep data from 2019 to 2021.
  • You need jobs from job boards and LinkedIn, not only from employers' career sites: TheirStack and Coresignal collect those.
  • You need updates within the hour: Fantastic.jobs refreshes hourly and TheirStack continuously; our feed is daily.
  • You sell to recruiters and need company data such as size and funding with each job: TheirStack and Coresignal attach it.
