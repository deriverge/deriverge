files

What your feed contains

Your feed lives at one private address that never changes. Every morning it holds:

  • open.xml: all open jobs of your feed in the Indeed XML job feed format, the format most job board importers read.
  • open.json and open.csv: the same jobs as a JSON array and a spreadsheet.
  • added and closed files for the day, in JSON Lines and CSV, if you prefer your own import script.

A job that closes at the employer drops out of open.xml the next morning, so a platform that removes missing jobs keeps your board clean on its own.

questions

Frequently asked questions

Which job board software can import the feed?

Any platform that imports jobs from an XML feed address: Cavuno, JBoard, SmartJobBoard, Jobboardly, JobBoard.io, Kardow, Jobiqo, Niceboard and WordPress sites with WP Job Manager and WP All Import Pro. The table above shows the plan each one needs.

What format is the feed?

XML in the Indeed job feed format, with extra elements for apply links, salary as separate values and remote or hybrid work. The same jobs come as JSON and CSV.

Can I test it without paying?

Yes. Point your importer at https://deriverge.com/tools/data/job-feed-sample.xml, a public sample of 40 jobs with descriptions. When you ask for a feed, the two-week trial uses your real feed.

Can I send these jobs on to Indeed?

Keep them on your own board. Indeed and some other aggregators do not accept jobs that were collected from other sites.
