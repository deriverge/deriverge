what you get

What is in the feed

Every open job our index finds on the public career sites of more than 66,000 companies, on 18 applicant tracking systems from Workday to Recruitee. The build of 9 November 2026 held 1,892,854 open jobs. Each job is one record with the fields below; there is no candidate data and no recruiter contact list.

niche feeds

Feeds picked for your audience

A job board for one audience needs the right jobs, not all of them. We build the selection with you and check every job before it reaches your feed:

  • Remote jobs open to one country. A job qualifies when its location names the country, or says Worldwide, Global or the region without naming another country, and its description does not limit the role to other countries (“must be based in the US”, “authorized to work in the UK”). Measured over 7 days in November 2026: from about 5 new jobs a day for Indonesia to 121 for the United States. 25 countries start online.
  • Jobs in one country or city, by job category, level or title keywords.
  • Jobs at the companies you name, or every job on one applicant tracking system.

In every feed we leave out talent pools and general applications, cap the new jobs from one employer per day so a large company does not fill your board, and remove email addresses and phone numbers from descriptions.

delivery

How the feed is delivered

  • Every morning, at a time you choose, your feed is updated at a private link that stays the same.
  • Three files in JSON Lines and CSV: the jobs added today, the jobs closed today, so you can take them down, and all open jobs of your feed.
  • A feed page shows today's new jobs and the download links, and manifest.json lists the same for your import script. Dated files stay available for 30 days.
  • Descriptions come as plain text and as clean HTML, ready to show on your site.
  • For job board software, all open jobs also come as an XML feed in the Indeed format and as a JSON array. Paste the address into your platform's job import and closed jobs drop out on their own. See which platforms read it.
  • On request, a signed webhook after every delivery: a POST with the counts and file links, so your import starts on its own.

faq

Questions

Where do the jobs come from?

From the public job boards that employers run on their applicant tracking systems, such as Workday, Greenhouse or Lever. Most career sites on our list were found through their addresses in the public Common Crawl web archive.

How fresh is the feed?

The index is rebuilt every night. Boards on ten systems are read daily and the other four in rotation within one to three days, so a new posting reaches the feed within three days.

Does the feed contain personal data?

No candidate data and no lists of recruiters. Descriptions are the employer's own text and can name a hiring manager or a contact; leave descriptions out if you do not need them. More on data sources and privacy.

Can I show the jobs on my own site?

You decide how you use the data and are responsible for using it lawfully and in line with the employers' terms. Every record links to the job and apply page on the employer's own site. We remove any company from your feed on request.
