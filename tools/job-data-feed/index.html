what you get

What is in the feed

Every open job our index finds on the public career sites of more than 47,000 companies, on 14 applicant tracking systems from Workday to Recruitee. The build of 19 October 2026 held 1,740,189 open jobs. Each job is one record with the fields below; there is no candidate data and no recruiter contact list.

delivery

How the feed is delivered

  • Every morning a gzipped JSON Lines file (CSV on request) with all open jobs of your feed, at a private download link.
  • Next to it, the jobs added and the jobs closed since the day before, so you can update your database without comparing files.
  • A filtered feed keeps only the countries, systems, job categories or title keywords you choose.

faq

Questions

Where do the jobs come from?

From the public job boards that employers run on their applicant tracking systems, such as Workday, Greenhouse or Lever. Most career sites on our list were found through their addresses in the public Common Crawl web archive.

How fresh is the feed?

The index is rebuilt every night. Boards on ten systems are read daily and the other four in rotation within one to three days, so a new posting reaches the feed within three days.

Does the feed contain personal data?

No candidate data and no lists of recruiters. Descriptions are the employer's own text and can name a hiring manager or a contact; leave descriptions out if you do not need them.

Can I show the jobs on my own site?

You decide how you use the data and are responsible for using it lawfully and in line with the employers' terms. Every record links to the job and apply page on the employer's own site. We remove any company from your feed on request.
