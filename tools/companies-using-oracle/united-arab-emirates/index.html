about the data

About Oracle Recruiting Cloud and this list

Oracle Recruiting Cloud is part of Oracle Fusion Cloud HCM, used by large enterprises, banks and public institutions. Career sites live on Oracle Cloud hosts such as <pod>.fa.<region>.oraclecloud.com.

How this list is made. We find Oracle Recruiting Cloud career sites in the public Common Crawl web archive and read each board's public job list every day for our job index. A company's country is the one where most of its open jobs are located, so a company that hires in several countries appears once, under its main one. Companies with no openings today have no known country and are left out here; they are in the full Oracle Recruiting Cloud list. This is a sample of employers we could find, not Oracle Recruiting Cloud's full customer list in the United Arab Emirates. For the jobs themselves, across all 21 systems, see job postings data for the United Arab Emirates.

The list holds company names and public career pages only, no personal data. If your company should not be here, write to info@deriverge.com.

Frequently asked questions

How many companies in the United Arab Emirates use Oracle Recruiting Cloud?

We know of 53 companies on Oracle Recruiting Cloud that hire mainly in the United Arab Emirates and had open jobs on 20 November 2026. The real number is higher: companies with no openings today, an unlisted board or most of their jobs in another country are not counted here.

Which companies in the United Arab Emirates use Oracle Recruiting Cloud?

By open jobs on 20 November 2026, the largest are Ali & Sons Holding LLC, Aster DM Healthcare Limited, LANDMARK GROUP, Lutech Resources, Apparel. The table above lists all 53 with a link to each careers page.

How is the country of a company decided?

We read the locations of all open jobs on the company's board and take the country that appears most often. A company that hires in several countries is listed once, under its main one.
