// why free

How these tools work

Every tool runs in your browser and talks directly to the public source: DNS resolvers, domain registries, the Internet Archive. There is no server of ours in between, no signup and no tracking of what you look up.

Each tool does one small job well. When you need thousands of rows, a schedule, an API or an export into your own systems, the same job runs as an actor in our Apify Store profile, where you pay only for the results you get.

Free tools by deriverge s.r.o., a software studio in the Czech Republic. Our data actors are in Apify Store.
