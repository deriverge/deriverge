about the data

About Recruitee and this list

Recruitee is collaborative hiring software from the Netherlands, used mostly by European small and mid-sized companies. Career sites live at <company>.recruitee.com, often behind the employer's own domain.

How this list is made. We find Recruitee career sites in the public Common Crawl web archive and read each board's public job list every day for our job index. A company's country is the one where most of its open jobs are located, so a company that hires in several countries appears once, under its main one. Companies with no openings today have no known country and are left out here; they are in the full Recruitee list. This is a sample of employers we could find, not Recruitee's full customer list in France. For the jobs themselves, across all 21 systems, see job postings data for France.

The list holds company names and public career pages only, no personal data. If your company should not be here, write to info@deriverge.com.

Frequently asked questions

How many companies in France use Recruitee?

We know of 70 companies on Recruitee that hire mainly in France and had open jobs on 20 November 2026. The real number is higher: companies with no openings today, an unlisted board or most of their jobs in another country are not counted here.

Which companies in France use Recruitee?

By open jobs on 20 November 2026, the largest are EIGHT ADVISORY SAS, RH Partners, Institut Mines-Télécom, AMI PARIS, HEMERIA. The table above lists all 70 with a link to each careers page.

How is the country of a company decided?

We read the locations of all open jobs on the company's board and take the country that appears most often. A company that hires in several countries is listed once, under its main one.

Can I download this list?

Yes. Open the full Recruitee list, where France is already selected, and press Download CSV. You get company, careers page, website where known, main country and open jobs. You may reuse the list under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 licence with a link to this page.

How do I get the open jobs of these companies?
