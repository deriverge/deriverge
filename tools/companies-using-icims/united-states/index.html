about the data

About iCIMS and this list

iCIMS is an enterprise talent acquisition platform used by large US employers, hospitals and universities. Employers run one or more portals at <portal>.icims.com. iCIMS lists rarely include locations, so the main country is often unknown.

How this list is made. We find iCIMS career sites in the public Common Crawl web archive and read each board's public job list every day for our job index. A company's country is the one where most of its open jobs are located, so a company that hires in several countries appears once, under its main one. Companies with no openings today have no known country and are left out here; they are in the full iCIMS list. This is a sample of employers we could find, not iCIMS's full customer list in the United States. For the jobs themselves, across all 21 systems, see job postings data for the United States.

The list holds company names and public career pages only, no personal data. If your company should not be here, write to info@deriverge.com.

Frequently asked questions

How many companies in the United States use iCIMS?

We know of 164 companies on iCIMS that hire mainly in the United States and had open jobs on 20 November 2026. The real number is higher: companies with no openings today, an unlisted board or most of their jobs in another country are not counted here.

Which companies in the United States use iCIMS?

By open jobs on 20 November 2026, the largest are Saint Joseph Medical Center - Joliet, Select Medical, Enterprise Holdings, Doherty Enterprises, The Cottonwood Hotel. The table above lists all 164 with a link to each careers page.

How is the country of a company decided?

We read the locations of all open jobs on the company's board and take the country that appears most often. A company that hires in several countries is listed once, under its main one.

Can I download this list?

Yes. Open the full iCIMS list, where United States is already selected, and press Download CSV. You get company, careers page, website where known, main country and open jobs. You may reuse the list under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 licence with a link to this page.

How do I get the open jobs of these companies?

Run a job scraper on Apify with the country filter set to United States: it searches all iCIMS jobs in our daily index and returns titles, locations, descriptions and apply links.
