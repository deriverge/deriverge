// check it yourself

How to tell if a company uses iCIMS

Open any job on the company's careers page and look where the Apply button leads. A company uses iCIMS when:

  • The portal is <portal>.icims.com, often careers-<company>.icims.com.
  • Job links look like /jobs/<id>/<title>/job.

iCIMS has no public job feed, so our index reads the job list pages of each portal.

Not sure which system a link belongs to? Paste it into the ATS detector, or search the company by name on the ATS overview.

// about the data

About iCIMS and this list

iCIMS is an enterprise talent acquisition platform used by large US employers, hospitals and universities. Employers run one or more portals at <portal>.icims.com. iCIMS lists rarely include locations, so the main country is often unknown.

How this list is made. We find iCIMS career sites in the public Common Crawl web archive and read each board's public job list every day for our job index. A company is listed when its board answered with a company name; companies with a live board but no openings today are included with 0 jobs. The main country is the country with most of the company's job locations. This is a sample of employers we could find, not iCIMS's full customer list. The list holds company names and public career pages only, no personal data. If your company should not be here, write to info@deriverge.com.

Frequently asked questions

How many companies use iCIMS?

We know of 1,969 companies with a public iCIMS career site, and 1,936 of them had open jobs on 19 October 2026. iCIMS's real customer count is higher: many employers keep their board unlisted or hire without a public board.

Which big companies use iCIMS?

By open jobs on 19 October 2026, the largest iCIMS employers in our data are Saint Joseph Medical Center - Joliet, Select Medical, Flanigan's Enterprises. The table above ranks the top 15 and the full list sorts by open jobs.

How can I tell if a company uses iCIMS?

Click Apply on one of its jobs and check the address. The portal is .icims.com, often careers-.icims.com.

Can I download the list of companies using iCIMS?

Yes. Download CSV above the table saves the rows that match your search and filters: company, careers page, website where known, main country and open jobs.
