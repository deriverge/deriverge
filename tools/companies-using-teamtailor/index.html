// check it yourself

How to tell if a company uses Teamtailor

Open any job on the company's careers page and look where the Apply button leads. A company uses Teamtailor when:

  • The career site is <company>.teamtailor.com/jobs, or a custom domain with jobs under /jobs/<id>-<title>.
  • The page source loads assets from teamtailor-cdn.com.

The public job feed of a Teamtailor board is at https://<company>.teamtailor.com/jobs.rss. It needs no key and is what our index reads every day.

Not sure which system a link belongs to? Paste it into the ATS detector, or search the company by name on the ATS overview.

// about the data

About Teamtailor and this list

Teamtailor is an employer branding and recruiting platform from Sweden, strongest in the Nordics. Career sites live at <company>.teamtailor.com or on the employer's own domain.

How this list is made. We find Teamtailor career sites in the public Common Crawl web archive and read each board's public job list every day for our job index. A company is listed when its board answered with a company name; companies with a live board but no openings today are included with 0 jobs. The main country is the country with most of the company's job locations. This is a sample of employers we could find, not Teamtailor's full customer list. The list holds company names and public career pages only, no personal data. If your company should not be here, write to info@deriverge.com.
