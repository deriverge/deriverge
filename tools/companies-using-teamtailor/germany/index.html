about the data

About Teamtailor and this list

Teamtailor is an employer branding and recruiting platform from Sweden, strongest in the Nordics. Career sites live at <company>.teamtailor.com or on the employer's own domain.

How this list is made. We find Teamtailor career sites in the public Common Crawl web archive and read each board's public job list every day for our job index. A company's country is the one where most of its open jobs are located, so a company that hires in several countries appears once, under its main one. Companies with no openings today have no known country and are left out here; they are in the full Teamtailor list. This is a sample of employers we could find, not Teamtailor's full customer list in Germany. For the jobs themselves, across all 21 systems, see job postings data for Germany.

The list holds company names and public career pages only, no personal data. If your company should not be here, write to info@deriverge.com.

Frequently asked questions

How many companies in Germany use Teamtailor?

We know of 46 companies on Teamtailor that hire mainly in Germany and had open jobs on 20 November 2026. The real number is higher: companies with no openings today, an unlisted board or most of their jobs in another country are not counted here.

Which companies in Germany use Teamtailor?

By open jobs on 20 November 2026, the largest are AniCura Group, Nachhilfeunterricht, Huawei Europe, TIP GROUP, Huawei Research Center Germany. The table above lists all 46 with a link to each careers page.

How is the country of a company decided?

We read the locations of all open jobs on the company's board and take the country that appears most often. A company that hires in several countries is listed once, under its main one.

Can I download this list?

Yes. Open the full Teamtailor list, where Germany is already selected, and press Download CSV. You get company, careers page, website where known, main country and open jobs. You may reuse the list under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 licence with a link to this page.

How do I get the open jobs of these companies?

Run a job scraper on Apify with the country filter set to Germany: it searches all Teamtailor jobs in our daily index and returns titles, locations, descriptions and apply links.
