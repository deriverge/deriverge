about the data

About Breezy HR and this list

Breezy HR is recruiting software for small businesses. Each company's career site lives at <company>.breezy.hr.

How this list is made. We find Breezy HR career sites in the public Common Crawl web archive and read each board's public job list every day for our job index. A company's country is the one where most of its open jobs are located, so a company that hires in several countries appears once, under its main one. Companies with no openings today have no known country and are left out here; they are in the full Breezy HR list. This is a sample of employers we could find, not Breezy HR's full customer list in the United Kingdom. For the jobs themselves, across all 21 systems, see job postings data for the United Kingdom.

The list holds company names and public career pages only, no personal data. If your company should not be here, write to info@deriverge.com.

Frequently asked questions

How many companies in the United Kingdom use Breezy HR?

We know of 67 companies on Breezy HR that hire mainly in the United Kingdom and had open jobs on 25 November 2026. The real number is higher: companies with no openings today, an unlisted board or most of their jobs in another country are not counted here.

Which companies in the United Kingdom use Breezy HR?

By open jobs on 25 November 2026, the largest are Reiss, Gold Care Homes, Marex, Integral, Incubeta. The table above lists all 67 with a link to each careers page.

How is the country of a company decided?

We read the locations of all open jobs on the company's board and take the country that appears most often. A company that hires in several countries is listed once, under its main one.

Can I download this list?

Yes. Open the full Breezy HR list, where United Kingdom is already selected, and press Download CSV. You get company, careers page, website where known, main country and open jobs. You may reuse the list under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 licence with a link to this page.

How do I get the open jobs of these companies?

Run a job scraper on Apify with the country filter set to United Kingdom: it searches all Breezy HR jobs in our daily index and returns titles, locations, descriptions and apply links.
