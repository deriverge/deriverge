// check it yourself

How to tell if a company uses Lever

Open any job on the company's careers page and look where the Apply button leads. A company uses Lever when:

  • Apply links go to jobs.lever.co/<company> or jobs.eu.lever.co/<company>.
  • Job links end with a long identifier, such as /<company>/0b1c2d3e-....

The public job feed of a Lever board is at https://api.lever.co/v0/postings/<company>?mode=json. It needs no key and is what our index reads every day.

Not sure which system a link belongs to? Paste it into the ATS detector, or search the company by name on the ATS overview.

// about the data

About Lever and this list

Lever is a recruiting platform from San Francisco that combines an applicant tracking system with a candidate CRM. Boards live at jobs.lever.co, or at jobs.eu.lever.co for accounts hosted in the European Union.

How this list is made. We find Lever career sites in the public Common Crawl web archive and read each board's public job list every day for our job index. A company is listed when its board answered with a company name; companies with a live board but no openings today are included with 0 jobs. The main country is the country with most of the company's job locations. This is a sample of employers we could find, not Lever's full customer list. The list holds company names and public career pages only, no personal data. If your company should not be here, write to info@deriverge.com.

How many companies use Lever?

We know of 1,710 companies with a public Lever career site, and 1,676 of them had open jobs on 11 October 2026. Lever's real customer count is higher: many employers keep their board unlisted or hire without a public board.

Which big companies use Lever?

By open jobs on 11 October 2026, the largest Lever employers in our data are TSMG, BoxLunch & Hot Topic, TowardJobs. The table above ranks the top 15 and the full list sorts by open jobs.

How can I tell if a company uses Lever?

Click Apply on one of its jobs and check the address. Apply links go to jobs.lever.co/ or jobs.eu.lever.co/.
