about the data

About Lever and this list

Lever is a recruiting platform from San Francisco that combines an applicant tracking system with a candidate CRM. Boards live at jobs.lever.co, or at jobs.eu.lever.co for accounts hosted in the European Union.

How this list is made. We find Lever career sites in the public Common Crawl web archive and read each board's public job list every day for our job index. A company's country is the one where most of its open jobs are located, so a company that hires in several countries appears once, under its main one. Companies with no openings today have no known country and are left out here; they are in the full Lever list. This is a sample of employers we could find, not Lever's full customer list in the United States. For the jobs themselves, across all 21 systems, see job postings data for the United States.

The list holds company names and public career pages only, no personal data. If your company should not be here, write to info@deriverge.com.

Frequently asked questions

How many companies in the United States use Lever?

We know of 973 companies on Lever that hire mainly in the United States and had open jobs on 25 November 2026. The real number is higher: companies with no openings today, an unlisted board or most of their jobs in another country are not counted here.

Which companies in the United States use Lever?

By open jobs on 25 November 2026, the largest are BoxLunch & Hot Topic, TSMG, TowardJobs, Contact Government Services, LLC, LifeStance Health. The table above lists the top 300 with a link to each careers page.

How is the country of a company decided?

We read the locations of all open jobs on the company's board and take the country that appears most often. A company that hires in several countries is listed once, under its main one.

Can I download this list?

Yes. Open the full Lever list, where United States is already selected, and press Download CSV. You get company, careers page, website where known, main country and open jobs. You may reuse the list under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 licence with a link to this page.

How do I get the open jobs of these companies?

Run a job scraper on Apify with the country filter set to United States: it searches all Lever jobs in our daily index and returns titles, locations, descriptions and apply links.
