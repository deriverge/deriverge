check it yourself

How to tell if a company uses Workday

Open any job on the company's careers page and look where the Apply button leads. A company uses Workday when:

  • Apply links go to <tenant>.wd1.myworkdayjobs.com/<site> (wd1, wd3, wd5, wd103 and others).
  • Job links contain /job/<location>/<title>_<requisition ID>.

The public job feed of a Workday board is at https://<tenant>.wd5.myworkdayjobs.com/wday/cxs/<tenant>/<site>/jobs (POST). It needs no key and is what our index reads every day.

Not sure which system a link belongs to? Paste it into the ATS detector, or search the company by name on the ATS overview.

about the data

About Workday and this list

Workday Recruiting is part of Workday's HR suite for large employers. Each employer has its own tenant at <tenant>.wd<N>.myworkdayjobs.com, often with several career sites per tenant, one per brand, region or audience. Workday names the legal entity of each job, so a group can appear under one of its subsidiaries. Where that entity does not match the career site, the brand from the site address comes first, as in Medtronic (CVS Covidien Sales LLC); the careers address always identifies the employer.

How this list is made. We find Workday career sites in the public Common Crawl web archive and read each board's public job list every day for our job index. A company is listed when its board answered with a company name; companies with a live board but no openings today are included with 0 jobs. The main country is the country with most of the company's job locations. This is a sample of employers we could find, not Workday's full customer list. The list holds company names and public career pages only, no personal data. If your company should not be here, write to info@deriverge.com.
