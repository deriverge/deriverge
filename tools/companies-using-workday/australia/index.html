about the data

About Workday and this list

Workday Recruiting is part of Workday's HR suite for large employers. Each employer has its own tenant at <tenant>.wd<N>.myworkdayjobs.com, often with several career sites per tenant, one per brand, region or audience. Workday names the legal entity of each job, so a group can appear under one of its subsidiaries. Where that entity does not match the career site, the brand from the site address comes first, as in Medtronic (CVS Covidien Sales LLC); the careers address always identifies the employer.

How this list is made. We find Workday career sites in the public Common Crawl web archive and read each board's public job list every day for our job index. A company's country is the one where most of its open jobs are located, so a company that hires in several countries appears once, under its main one. Companies with no openings today have no known country and are left out here; they are in the full Workday list. This is a sample of employers we could find, not Workday's full customer list in Australia. For the jobs themselves, across all 21 systems, see job postings data for Australia.

The list holds company names and public career pages only, no personal data. If your company should not be here, write to info@deriverge.com.

Frequently asked questions

How many companies in Australia use Workday?

We know of 34 companies on Workday that hire mainly in Australia and had open jobs on 20 November 2026. The real number is higher: companies with no openings today, an unlisted board or most of their jobs in another country are not counted here.

Which companies in Australia use Workday?

By open jobs on 20 November 2026, the largest are The Salvation Army (VIC) Prpty Trust atf Social Work, Sandvik (SMC Australia), Aurecon Australasia Pty Ltd, St Vincent's Care Services, Telstra Limited. The table above lists all 34 with a link to each careers page.

How is the country of a company decided?

We read the locations of all open jobs on the company's board and take the country that appears most often. A company that hires in several countries is listed once, under its main one.

Can I download this list?

Yes. Open the full Workday list, where Australia is already selected, and press Download CSV. You get company, careers page, website where known, main country and open jobs. You may reuse the list under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 licence with a link to this page.

How do I get the open jobs of these companies?

Run a job scraper on Apify with the country filter set to Australia: it searches all Workday jobs in our daily index and returns titles, locations, descriptions and apply links.
