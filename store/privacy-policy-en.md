Tapkasa is a point-of-sale app for recording sales at stalls and events.
This policy describes, completely and truthfully, how the app handles data.
The short version: **no accounts, no analytics, no ads, no tracking.
Your sales data stays on your device.**

## 1. What the app does not collect

- It neither requires nor offers account creation. It does not know your
  name, e-mail address or phone number.
- It contains no third-party analytics, advertising or marketing tools and
  collects no usage statistics.
- It does not access your location, contacts, photos or any other data on
  your device.
- It does not process payments or payment details. You take cash and card
  payments outside the app (cards on your own card terminal); Tapkasa only
  records that a sale happened. Your customers' card details never pass
  through the app.
- It records no data about your customers. A receipt in the app contains
  only item names, quantities, prices, the payment method (cash/card) and
  a timestamp.

## 2. Data stored on your device

The app writes exclusively to your device's storage:

- your product list with prices and colours, the event name and settings,
- saved receipts, the order queue and the archive of closed events.

None of this is transmitted anywhere (the only exception is optional
pairing, see section 3). The app keeps several copies inside its own
storage so a restart or a dead battery cannot destroy them. You are in
full control:

- **Deletion:** items and receipts can be deleted directly in the app;
  uninstalling the app permanently deletes all of its data.
- **Backup:** the "Data backup" feature shows the till's contents as plain
  text that you copy wherever you choose. Where you keep that backup is
  entirely up to you; the operator has no access to it.

## 3. Optional pairing of two devices

You can pair two of your own devices (roles "till" and "pickup") so they
share the order queue. Pairing is **off until you enable it yourself** by
creating or entering a pairing code.

While pairing is on, the devices exchange **order contents only**: item
names, prices, quantities, ticket numbers, order totals, the payment
method (cash/card), served status, timestamps and the event name. The
messages also carry the randomly generated pairing code and a random
per-session device identifier. **They contain no personal data** — no
names, no contacts, no location, nothing about your customers.

Messages travel over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) through a relay
server that delivers them to the paired device. The relay retains messages
briefly so the other device can pick them up even after a connection
drop — **for at most 12 hours**, after which they are deleted. The app
operator does not read, store or have access to these messages. You can
end pairing at any time with the "Disconnect" button; from that moment
nothing is sent.

## 4. The Tapkasa Pro subscription

Subscriptions are purchased and billed exclusively through the **Apple
App Store** or **Google Play**, depending on your platform. Payment
details (card numbers etc.) are processed by Apple or Google under their
own privacy policies; the operator of Tapkasa never sees them.

To verify whether a subscription is active, the app uses **RevenueCat**
(RevenueCat, Inc.), which processes the App Store / Google Play purchase
receipt together with a **random, anonymous identifier generated by the
app**. This identifier is not linked to your name, e-mail address or any
other information about you. RevenueCat acts as a processor; its policy:
https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

## 5. Legal basis and your rights (GDPR)

The app is designed so that the operator collects no personal data about
its users. Sales data exists only on your device, where you manage and
delete it yourself — which places the rights of access, rectification and
erasure directly in your own hands. The only data processed off-device is:

- transient pairing messages (section 3) — free of personal data, deleted
  within 12 hours; legal basis: performance of a contract (providing the
  feature you switched on),
- the anonymous identifier and purchase receipt used to verify the
  subscription (section 4); legal basis: performance of a contract.

For any privacy question or request, contact [DOPLŇ: support e-mail].
You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory
authority — in the Czech Republic, the Office for Personal Data
Protection (Úřad pro ochranu osobních údajů, www.uoou.gov.cz), or the
authority in your own EU member state.

## 6. Children

The app is a work tool for sellers and is not directed at children. It
collects no data about users of any age.
