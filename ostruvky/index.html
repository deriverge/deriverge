3Wachsen

Ruhige Aufgaben wünschen sich einen größeren Wald, ein Feld, ein Dorf oder einen See. Dafür gibt es Extra-Plättchen.

A calm 3D tile-laying game

Build a little island, one tile at a time

Each hexagon tile carries a piece of landscape. Turn it so the edges match its neighbours and watch houses, windmills, trees and boats appear on their own.

Coming soon to the App Store and Google Play

How to play

1Place

Tap a glowing spot next to your island. The tile turns to fit as well as it can.

2Match

Every edge that matches scores. Match all six sides for a perfect tile and an extra tile.

3Grow

Gentle tasks ask for a bigger forest, field, village or lake. Finish them for bonus tiles.

Four ways to play

Classic

Grow your island until the tiles run out and chase your best score.

Journey

30 isles with different landscapes and stars to earn.

Daily Isle

The same island for everyone today. Missed a day? No problem.

Relax

Endless tiles and a free tile swap. Just build.

Fair and relaxing, by design

  • No timers, no energy, no lives
  • Free hints and undo
  • Ads only between finished islands, never mid-game, and only rarely
  • Videos only when you ask for them
  • One-time purchases only, no subscriptions or loot boxes
  • Plays offline, in 35 languages

Seasons to unlock

Spring, summer, autumn, winter, cherry blossom and evening, all unlocked just by playing.
