What Tapkasa does

Sell in three taps

Every item is a colour card. Tap to add, hold to remove. Any helper can run it without training.

Cash and card

For cash, Tapkasa counts the change. Cards go through your own terminal, the app just records the sale.

Order queue

Paid orders line up with numbers. The prep counter ticks them off, nobody waits twice.

Two devices, no internet

Till and prep counter connect directly over Wi-Fi or a hotspot. Works in a field or a cellar.

Revenue and documents

Per-item revenue, cash and card split, and a summary sales PDF with a VAT breakdown.

Your data stays yours

No accounts, no cloud. Everything lives on your device; a backup is text you keep wherever you like.

Made for

  • Coffee or drinks stalls at markets and festivals: fast menu, order queue, till + prep pairing.
  • School fairs and community events: volunteers can run it, and the evening count is exact.
  • Pop-ups and seasonal sales: set up in a minute, no monthly fee for the basics.

FAQ

How much does it cost?

The core is free: full menu, 10 paid bills a day, reports and documents. Tapkasa Pro unlocks unlimited billing and device pairing, with a 14-day free trial.

Is it a certified fiscal register?

No. Tapkasa records and counts sales; it does not process payments. For stalls and occasional selling that is usually all you need; check your local rules.

Does it work offline?

Yes, everything runs on the device, including two-device pairing.

Languages?

English, Czech, Slovak, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Polish and Portuguese. Any currency.
