Christmas markets, craft fairs, winter fêtes

A till for Christmas markets and craft fairs

The square is packed, the mobile network is struggling and there is a queue at your stall. Tapkasa runs on your phone with no internet, works out the change and keeps track of mug deposits. You can set it up in a few minutes.

Mug deposits without the muddle

In Menu, tap “+ Add deposit” and enter the amount. Tapkasa creates two items: Deposit and Deposit return. Add the deposit with the mulled wine, tap the return when the mug comes back and it is taken off the bill. If a customer is only returning a mug, Tapkasa shows how much to hand back.

The totals list deposits taken and returned separately, so at closing time you know how many mugs are still out and what your real sales were.

When the signal drops

Selling, change and totals all run on the phone itself. Tapkasa does not log in anywhere and does not check anything online, so a busy network or a used-up data plan cannot stop you in the middle of the rush.

Handmade goods and one-off prices

Save your regular items as coloured cards. For a one-off piece that is not on the menu, tap “+ Other amount”, type the price and carry on. At the end of the day the overview shows what sold and how many.

Tips for a cold day

  • Round your prices so change is easy.
  • Set up the menu at home in the warm and put your best sellers at the top.
  • Bring a power bank. The screen stays on while you sell, and batteries drain faster in the cold.
  • Save a backup file before your first market day.

How it works

  1. Type in your menu

    Each item gets a name, a price and a colour. It takes a few minutes at home, or start from the sample menu.

  2. Tap the cards

    A tap adds one, a long press takes one off. The amount due is always at the bottom.

  3. Cash or card

    For cash, Tapkasa works out the change. Take cards on your own reader and just mark the sale as card in Tapkasa.

What Tapkasa does

Change without mental maths

Tap the note you were handed and see what to give back.

Numbered order queue

Every paid bill gets a number and waits in the queue until you mark it as served.

Till and pickup on two phones

A second device shows the orders and can read them aloud. Linking devices is part of Tapkasa Pro.

Works with no signal

Everything runs on the phone. Two iPhones or two Android phones link up even without internet.

Refundable deposits

Your data stays with you

No account and no cloud. Save a backup file wherever you like.

What Tapkasa looks like

What Tapkasa is not

  • It is not a certified or fiscal cash register and it reports nothing to tax authorities. The sales summary is not a tax invoice or a customer receipt.
  • It does not process card payments. You take cards on your own reader and Tapkasa only records the sale.
  • It does not print receipts or tickets.

For stalls, fairs and occasional selling that is usually all you need. Check with your accountant or tax office which rules apply to you.

What it costs

Free: the whole menu, totals, deposits and data backup on one device, with 10 paid bills a day. A bill that only returns a deposit does not count towards the limit.

Tapkasa Pro: unlimited bills and linking the till with a pickup device. It is a monthly or yearly subscription through the App Store or Google Play, with a 14-day free trial for new subscribers. You see the price in your currency in the app before you buy, and you can cancel any time in your store account.

One subscription is enough for a stall: the second phone joins a device with Tapkasa Pro for free.

Questions people ask

Do deposit returns count towards the free daily limit?

No. A bill that only returns a deposit does not count towards the 10 paid bills a day.

Is the free version enough for a Christmas market?

For a small stall with a handful of sales a day, yes. For a busy market Tapkasa Pro makes sense: new subscribers get the first 14 days free, which covers two market weekends. Tapkasa warns you before you reach the limit.

Is Tapkasa free?

Yes, for up to 10 paid bills a day on one device. Unlimited bills and linking two devices need the Tapkasa Pro subscription, which new subscribers can try free for 14 days.

Is it a certified or fiscal cash register?

No. Tapkasa counts and records your sales, but it is not a certified register, sends nothing to tax authorities and does not issue tax invoices.

Where is my data?

Only on your device. Tapkasa has no accounts and no cloud. You can save a backup file and load it on a new phone.

Which languages and currencies does it support?

Nine languages (English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Polish, Czech and Slovak) and 33 currencies, each with its own notes for working out change.
