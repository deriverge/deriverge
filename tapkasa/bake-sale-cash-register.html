A cash register for bake sales, school fairs and fundraisers

Volunteers take turns at the table and the treasurer wants to know what came in. Tapkasa keeps the prices, works out the change and adds up the takings on a phone. No sign-up and no training.

Any volunteer can run it

Items are big coloured cards, so nobody scrolls through a list or does sums in their head. Tap what the customer asked for, choose Cash or Card, and for cash the screen shows the change. Changing shifts means handing over the phone.

Exact totals for the treasurer

The overview shows total sales, cash and card separately, and how many of each item were sold. Send the sales summary to the treasurer or the committee as a PDF. After the event, tap Close event: the takings go to the archive and next time you start from zero.

No account, no verification, no fees

Tapkasa asks for no registration, no charity number and no bank details, and it takes no cut of what you raise. The money goes into your cash box. The data stays on the phone you use at the table.

Bigger events: bar and kitchen

At a dance or a summer fair one phone can take payments at the bar while another shows the orders in the kitchen. One Tapkasa Pro subscription covers it, and new subscribers get a 14-day free trial.

How it works

  1. Type in your menu

    Each item gets a name, a price and a colour. It takes a few minutes at home, or start from the sample menu.

  2. Tap the cards

    A tap adds one, a long press takes one off. The amount due is always at the bottom.

  3. Cash or card

    For cash, Tapkasa works out the change. Take cards on your own reader and just mark the sale as card in Tapkasa.

What Tapkasa does

Change without mental maths

Tap the note you were handed and see what to give back.

Numbered order queue

Every paid bill gets a number and waits in the queue until you mark it as served.

Till and pickup on two phones

A second device shows the orders and can read them aloud. Linking devices is part of Tapkasa Pro.

Works with no signal

Everything runs on the phone. Two iPhones or two Android phones link up even without internet.

Refundable deposits

A cup or mug deposit and its return as separate items, kept apart from your sales in the totals.

Your data stays with you

No account and no cloud. Save a backup file wherever you like.

What Tapkasa is not

  • It is not a certified or fiscal cash register and it reports nothing to tax authorities. The sales summary is not a tax invoice or a customer receipt.
  • It does not process card payments. You take cards on your own reader and Tapkasa only records the sale.
  • It does not print receipts or tickets.

For stalls, fairs and occasional selling that is usually all you need. Check with your accountant or tax office which rules apply to you.

Questions people ask

Is the free version enough for a school bake sale?

For a small sale, yes: 10 paid bills a day are free. If you expect more customers, start Tapkasa Pro beforehand; new subscribers get a 14-day free trial.

Does our club or PTA need to register?

No. You download Tapkasa and open it. There is no account to create and nothing to verify.

Is Tapkasa free?

Yes, for up to 10 paid bills a day on one device. Unlimited bills and linking two devices need the Tapkasa Pro subscription, which new subscribers can try free for 14 days.

Is it a certified or fiscal cash register?

No. Tapkasa counts and records your sales, but it is not a certified register, sends nothing to tax authorities and does not issue tax invoices.

Where is my data?

Only on your device. Tapkasa has no accounts and no cloud. You can save a backup file and load it on a new phone.

Which languages and currencies does it support?

Nine languages (English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Polish, Czech and Slovak) and 33 currencies, each with its own notes for working out change.
