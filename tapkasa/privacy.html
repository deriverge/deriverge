Privacy Policy

Tapkasa · effective 31 Aug 2026 · operator: deriverge s.r.o. · contact: info@deriverge.com

Tapkasa is a point-of-sale app that records sales at stands and events. Short version: no accounts, no analytics, no ads, no tracking. Your sales data stays on your device.

1. What the app does not collect

  • No account, the app doesn't know your name, e-mail or phone number.
  • No third-party analytics, advertising or marketing tools.
  • No location, contacts or photos access.
  • No payment processing and no payment data, you take cash and cards outside the app; Tapkasa only records the sale. Your customers' card details never pass through the app.
  • No data about your customers. A receipt holds only items, quantities, prices, payment method and time.

2. Data on your device

Your menu, settings, receipts, order queue and archives are stored only on your device and are never sent anywhere (the one exception is optional pairing below). Deleting them in the app, or uninstalling it, removes them permanently; the “Data backup” feature shows them as text you can copy wherever you choose.

3. Optional device pairing

Paired devices (till + prep counter) exchange order contents only: item names, quantities, prices, order numbers and served status, never settings, archives or anything about your device. Messages travel encrypted (HTTPS) through a relay server under a random pairing code, and the relay keeps them for at most 12 hours before deleting them. In the native app, devices can also connect directly (Bonjour/Multipeer) with no internet, then no server is used at all. Pairing is off until you turn it on yourself.

4. Tapkasa Pro subscription

Purchases are handled by the app store under its terms; the operator never sees your payment details. To validate the purchase the app uses RevenueCat with an anonymous, app-generated identifier, no name, no e-mail, no cross-app link. Your subscription purchase history is the only data processed this way.

5. Your rights

Because data lives on your device, you delete and export it yourself, directly in the app. For privacy questions, contact info@deriverge.com.
