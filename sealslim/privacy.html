Privacy Policy

SealSlim · effective 5 October 2026 · operator: deriverge s.r.o. · contact: info@deriverge.com

SealSlim makes smaller copies of your photos right on your phone or tablet. Short version: your photos never leave your device. No account, no analytics, no ads, no tracking.

1. Your photos stay on your device

  • All compression runs on your device, even offline. Your photos are never uploaded, not to us and not to anyone else.
  • The app sees only the photos you pick in the system photo picker, never the rest of your library.
  • SealSlim always makes new copies. Your originals are never changed or deleted.
  • By default, location (GPS) and other hidden metadata (EXIF, such as camera and date) are removed from the copies.
  • A copy leaves your device only if you share it yourself, for example by e-mail, in a messenger, or to Files or Drive. It then goes to the app or service you chose, under its own terms.

2. Permissions

  • iPhone and iPad: picking photos uses the system picker and needs no permission. To save copies, the app asks for permission to add photos to your library. This add-only access lets it add new photos; it cannot see your library.
  • Android 13 and newer: picking photos uses the system photo picker and needs no permission.
  • Android 9 and older: the app asks for storage permission, used only to save the copies.

3. What the app does not collect

  • No account: the app does not know your name, e-mail address or phone number.
  • No analytics, no ads, no tracking, and no third-party tools that collect data, apart from the purchase check described below.
  • We do not sell any data and do not share any data for advertising.
  • Apple and Google may give us anonymous, aggregated statistics and crash reports from people who allowed sharing with developers in their device settings. The app itself does not collect them.

4. Purchases (SealSlim Pro)

SealSlim Pro is a one-time in-app purchase. Payment is processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) under their own terms and privacy policies. We never see your payment details.

To verify your purchase and restore it (for example after reinstalling the app or on a new device), the app uses RevenueCat, a service of RevenueCat, Inc. RevenueCat receives only the store's purchase receipt or token and a random, anonymous app user ID created by the app: no name, no e-mail address, no photos. RevenueCat, Inc. is based in the USA, so this data may be processed there. The legal basis is performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). More at revenuecat.com/privacy.

5. How long data is kept

  • SealSlim keeps no copies of your photos anywhere outside your device. Copies you save stay in your photo library until you delete them.
  • On your device, the app keeps only a little technical data, such as how many free photos you used today and whether you own Pro. Uninstalling the app deletes it, together with any temporary files.
  • RevenueCat keeps the purchase record as long as it is needed to verify and restore your purchase. You can ask us to delete it.
  • If you e-mail us, we keep your message only as long as needed to handle your request.

6. Children

SealSlim is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

7. Your rights

The controller of your personal data is deriverge s.r.o. (info@deriverge.com). Under the GDPR you have the right to access your personal data, to have it corrected or erased, to restrict or object to its processing, and to data portability. You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection authority, for example in your country or, in the Czech Republic, with the Office for Personal Data Protection (ÚOOÚ).

Your photos and app data are on your device, so you control them directly. For anything else, including the purchase record at RevenueCat, write to info@deriverge.com. Because we do not know who you are, we may ask for details from your store receipt to find the right record.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, we will publish the new version on this page with a new effective date.

6. Kinderen

SealSlim is niet bedoeld voor kinderen jonger dan 13 jaar. We verzamelen niet bewust persoonsgegevens van kinderen.

7. Je rechten

De verwerkingsverantwoordelijke voor je persoonsgegevens is deriverge s.r.o. (info@deriverge.com). Op grond van de AVG heb je recht op inzage in je persoonsgegevens, op rectificatie of wissing, op beperking van de verwerking, het recht van bezwaar en het recht op overdraagbaarheid van gegevens. Je kunt ook een klacht indienen bij een toezichthouder, bijvoorbeeld in je eigen land of bij de Tsjechische toezichthouder (ÚOOÚ).

Je foto's en app-gegevens staan op je apparaat, dus je hebt ze zelf in de hand. Voor al het andere, ook voor de aankoopgegevens bij RevenueCat, mail je naar info@deriverge.com. Omdat we niet weten wie je bent, kunnen we je vragen om gegevens van je aankoopbewijs om de juiste gegevens te vinden.
